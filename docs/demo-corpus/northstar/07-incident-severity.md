# Northstar Systems Incident Severity Levels

Category: Incident Response
Version: NS-IR-1.9
Updated: 2026-08-21
Demo note: Northstar Systems is a fictional organization for the GroundStack portfolio demo.

## Severity One

Classify an incident as **Severity One** when a production service is unavailable for most employees or customers, data integrity is at immediate risk, or a security control is actively bypassed. Severity One requires an incident commander, live response channel, and executive notification within 15 minutes.

## Severity Two

Use **Severity Two** when a major feature is degraded, a workaround exists, and data integrity is not at immediate risk. Notify the service owner and support lead within 30 minutes.

## Severity Three

Use **Severity Three** for localized defects, delayed jobs, or minor performance degradation affecting a limited group.

## Required Fields

Every incident record must include impact summary, start time, affected services, severity, commander, and next update time.

## Review

Severity One and Two incidents require a post-incident review within five business days.
