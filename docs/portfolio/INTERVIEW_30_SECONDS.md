# 30-Second Interview Pitch

GroundStack is an AI technical-support app I built as a release-candidate portfolio project. It
ingests docs, retrieves evidence with hybrid search and reranking, streams LLaMA-compatible answers,
and validates citations so unsupported answers are rejected or clearly abstain. I also built the
platform pieces around it: auth boundaries, migrations, metrics, evaluation, feedback review,
training-data preparation, Discord slash commands, synthetic load-test evidence, and final release
audit docs. I’m careful to distinguish what was tested locally from what would require production
deployment evidence.
